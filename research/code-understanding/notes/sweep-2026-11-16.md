### C. Rasoi — #7 and #17

- **Paper.** "Rasoi: Visual Diffs for Evaluating Structural Changes Made by Coding Agents." Sahni
  et al., Columbia, including Lydia B. Chilton. [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16293). Submitted
  12 Nov 2026. v1 is 2026-11-12T04:47:39Z. Categories on the API entry: cs.SE, and cs.HC.
- **What the abstract says.** A design probe maps how a codebase's architecture changes after each
  agent edit. It shows architectural elements and relationships added, modified, or removed, at
  several abstraction levels. Week-long study, 12 developers, their own codebases. Visual diffs
  helped them see whether changes stayed in scope, notice unexpected restructuring, and relate
  changed parts to the whole. The authors say oversight tools matter more because developers gain
  confidence in their own evaluation than because they trust the agent. No comprehension quiz is
  reported in the abstract.
- **Evidence:** `measured-obs` (qualitative field study).
- **Use.** #7, the structural map of what a change is wired into. #17, a per-edit delta. The #17
  item body is unchanged. That item is seven lines, and open PRs #19, #21, #23, and #25 each insert
  on a different one of them. The #17 cite is in the 2026-11-16 changes section. The study does not
  move #7. There is no quiz score to rank against #1.
